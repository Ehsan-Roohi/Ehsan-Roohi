## Video preparation

FUM copies retain the presentation panel at source coordinates x=0, y=0, width=768, height=736, permanently removing the right sidebar with student webcams, participant names and chat, plus the old playback bar. ERAU copies retain the slide/narrator image and use a maximum width of 1280 pixels and 12 fps. Copies use H.264 video and mono AAC audio. Originals remain unchanged outside the publication folder.

The manifest records source/derivative SHA-256 digests and processing details. Numbered segments retain the complete edited audio/video packet streams, verified by SHA-256 packet hashes; durations are compared against the originals. Posters come from edited copies. No speech or lecture content is synthesized. Original pauses, connection screens, scan orientation and audio limitations remain attributable to the source.

## Historical limits

No separate Summer 2025 syllabus was found; Fall 2024 policies are not assigned to Summer. No syllabus was found inside the Fall 2022 folder. The revision named “1401” is included with its original source location explained. Fall 2024 lists Homework 11 in the schedule, but only HW1–HW10 were found. ## Selection and reuse

The publication contains teaching documents and learning resources. Rosters, grades, student submissions, private feedback, full textbook copies, publisher solution manuals and standalone homework answer keys are outside this selection. Original credits remain. No universal license is asserted for third-party components; cite the instructor/course and retain the original credits.
