The course introduces liquid and solid propulsion, single and multistage vehicle performance, combustion thermochemistry, nuclear rockets and electric propulsion. It prepares students for propulsion and astronautics preliminary and detailed design courses.

## Learning outcomes

The original syllabus expects students to examine mission requirements and propulsion-system choices; explain propulsion terminology; calculate combustion chemistry and nozzle flow; size systems and subcomponents; analyze component limitations; and appreciate the range of propulsion options and technical disciplines involved.

## Books

The Fall 2024 required text is Philip G. Hill and Carl R. Peterson, *Mechanics and Thermodynamics of Propulsion*, 1992. The optional text is Stephen D. Heister, William E. Anderson, Timothée L. Pourpoint and R. Joseph Cassady, *Rocket Propulsion*, Cambridge University Press, 2019. See [materials and official resources](MATERIALS.md).
