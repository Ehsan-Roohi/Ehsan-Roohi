## NASA CEA

The original course guide and equilibrium worksheet document the classroom workflow. For current access and documentation use the official [CEARUN interface](https://cearun.grc.nasa.gov/) and [CEARUN introduction](https://cearun.grc.nasa.gov/intro.html). The [NASA CEA tutorial](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240016039) is an additional official reference.

Retain the reactant definitions and states, chamber pressure, mixture-ratio definition, nozzle pressure or area ratio, equilibrium or frozen-flow setting, units and software/database version. An output from a current version may differ from an archived classroom result.

## Reading

- Philip G. Hill and Carl R. Peterson. *Mechanics and Thermodynamics of Propulsion*. 1992. Required in the Fall 2024 syllabus.
- Stephen D. Heister, William E. Anderson, Timothée L. Pourpoint and R. Joseph Cassady. [*Rocket Propulsion*](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/rocket-propulsion/4EC93E205DECCFF78FA4D1C8A0748BF2). Cambridge University Press, 2019. Optional in the original syllabus.
- NASA Glenn. [Thrust equation](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/thrust-force/) and [specific impulse](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/specific-impulse/).

Publisher textbook PDFs and instructor solution manuals are not redistributed. Textbook-linked question sheets refer to the original books, and figures retain their original attribution.
