## Recording format

Complete lecture duration and audio are retained. Three recordings are supplied as two consecutive MP4 segments. Splitting uses stream copy; compressed audio and video packet hashes match the complete recording. The videos use H.264 at 1280-pixel width and 12 frames per second with AAC mono audio. Source and publication checksums and processing details are in [manifest.json](manifest.json). Original local files remain unchanged.

The Week 10 recap covers solid rocket motors. The method-of-characteristics recording accompanies nozzle design. The additional classroom recording blacks out the student seating area and the standing student at the start; the original audio is retained.
