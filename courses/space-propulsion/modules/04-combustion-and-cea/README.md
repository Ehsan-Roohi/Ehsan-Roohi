# 04 Combustion chemistry and NASA CEA

[Course home](../../README.md) · [All lectures](../../LECTURES.md) · [Assignments](../../ASSIGNMENTS.md)

How does mixture ratio change equilibrium composition and rocket performance?

## Study goals

- Distinguish stoichiometric mixture ratio from the mixture ratio giving maximum performance.
- Record pressure, reactant state, units and equilibrium or frozen assumptions in a CEA calculation.
