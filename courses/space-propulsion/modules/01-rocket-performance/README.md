# 01 Rocket performance and staging

[Course home](../../README.md) · [All lectures](../../LECTURES.md) · [Assignments](../../ASSIGNMENTS.md)

How do thrust, mass ratio and staging determine the velocity a rocket can deliver?

## Study goals

- Distinguish exhaust velocity from effective exhaust velocity and specific impulse.
- Use mass ratios and loss assumptions consistently in single-stage and multistage calculations.
