# 05 Liquid rocket engines

[Course home](../../README.md) · [All lectures](../../LECTURES.md) · [Assignments](../../ASSIGNMENTS.md)

How do propellants, feed systems and engine cycles fit together?

## Study goals

- Trace mass and energy through pressure-fed and turbopump-fed systems.
- Compare propellant choices, tank volume, pump power, injectors and cavitation limits.
