# Source provenance and reuse

This archive presents Ehsan Roohi Golkhatmi's AE 414 teaching materials from Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University, Fall 2024 and Spring 2025. [Teaching record](https://websites.umass.edu/roohie/teaching/).

## Preservation

Original lecture PDFs, question sheets and supporting documents are copied without changing their contents. Filenames and folders are standardized for navigation. Original relative source paths, semester, file size, page count where available and SHA-256 checksum are recorded in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

Recordings are complete compressed derivatives of Fall 2024 source videos. The manifest records both the source and published checksums, duration and conversion settings. Recordings are grouped by topic and the week named in the source; the navigation order is not a claim about their exact recording dates. No Spring 2025 recordings were found.

## Interpretation of the archive

- The Fall 2024 syllabus and lecture outline establish the main course sequence.
- The 14 Fall 2024 numbered sheets are an exercise bank, not a reconstructed chronological assignment register. Sheet 14 is absent.
- Spring 2025 has 11 numbered homework PDFs and its own annotated note parts.
- A Spring 2025 syllabus was not found; no grading policy or deadline has been inferred from the earlier term.
- Nuclear propulsion is a companion topic, not an invented week in the historical schedule.

Navigation text, topic summaries and the course diagram were prepared with AI assistance from the available files. The original technical teaching files remain unchanged. Publication of an archived calculation or hint does not imply that its mathematical assumptions or result have been newly validated.

## Reuse and attribution

Credit the instructor, course, institution and semester when citing the teaching materials. This archive does not add a blanket MIT or Creative Commons license to the original notes. Embedded textbook figures, excerpts and other third-party material retain their respective rights. Permission for reuse beyond applicable exceptions should be obtained from the relevant rights holder.

Student submissions, scored papers, private feedback and class photographs are omitted. At the instructor’s request, the additional classroom recording is included with student image areas blacked out. Three recordings are split into two consecutive MP4 segments; stream-copy packet hashes verify that splitting preserves both the edited video and audio streams. Standalone examination solutions, answer keys and publisher book files are also omitted.
